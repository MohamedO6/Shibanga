## Scientific boundary retained

The game's island, bridge, survey A/B and gauge readings are fictional. A bridge powered by pairing dates is a game mechanic,
not a physical claim about NISAR. Field notes teach scientific habits, not operational flood response.
The real Delta composites are qualitative provisional HH/HV previews. Their colours do not validate a crop or flood classifier.
The actual Central Valley comparison remains about 3.06 cm residual RMS, versus 0.426 cm for a zero-motion baseline;
the small-motion sample is unresolved. Historical ALX2 ends before the 2026 radar images. The game does not change those facts.

## Three remaining readiness gates

1. **Eligibility:** Fringe and this addition predate the 14–15 November event. The 2026 Santiago Local Event page on the
   official platform explicitly distinguishes preparation from starting a competition project before the hackathon:
   https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/local-events/santiago-dominican-republic/ . This is Local Lead content, not a
   substitute for central clarification. Preserve the true history and obtain organizer clarification about eligible reuse.
   This work is local product development, not evidence that the project is an eligible competition entry. No inquiry was sent.
2. **Actual user evidence:** run the appended game protocol in USER_STUDY.md. Report completion, help and misconceptions
   for real participants. Browser QA is engineering verification, not a usability study.
3. **Scientific evidence:** before claiming measured water-change accuracy or operational value, define and execute a small
   reproducible quantitative NISAR case with an appropriate independent, date-compatible reference. Do not optimize by hiding
   negative comparisons, and do not relabel browse images as calibrated measurements.

## Reviewable demo sequence

Show 15–20 seconds of genuine movement and radar discovery, the two-date world switch, and the completed evidence pack.
State that the training case is fictional. Show the actual dated NISAR preview in the finale; change a date and enter the map.
Use Timeline, Layers and Details & Sources. Export an observation report. Show the unresolved station comparison if discussing
accuracy. Close with the specific user task and observed evidence, not a guaranteed prize or untested impact claim.

The existing code and cinematic journey are retained. No public deployment, competition submission, external communication,
new scientific processing or independent user study was performed as part of this game implementation.
